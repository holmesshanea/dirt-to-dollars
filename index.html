Welcome to Dirt to Dollars

Explore three fictional detecting properties, recover hidden metal objects, sell common finds for equipment, and keep your favorite discoveries as trophies.

  1. Walk with WASD or arrow keys.
  2. Listen for beeps and watch the detector display.
  3. Move slowly across a signal, then press P to pinpoint.
  4. When near the target, press E or Space to dig.
  5. Scan the soil with the handheld pinpointer, then recover the object.
  6. Sell ordinary finds to buy upgrades or save rare discoveries as trophies.

There is no overall time limit. Battery management is your only time-related constraint.

Controls and shortcuts

W / A / S / D or arrow keys: walk. P: toggle detector pinpoint. E or Space: dig. H: open Help Center.

Touchscreen players can use the directional buttons below the game. Use the detector panel buttons to pinpoint and dig.

The detector sweeps automatically. Press the Sound button to turn audio on or off.

When a dialog is open, the game pauses. Close the dialog to continue exploring.

Understanding detector signals

The large number is a target ID, an approximate indication of conductivity—not a guarantee of what is buried. Iron often produces low, broken tones; many coins produce higher tones. Aluminum and gold can overlap.

Signal strength increases as the coil approaches the object. Stability reflects how consistently the detector identifies it. Depth bars indicate estimated burial depth.

Deep targets, nearby iron, wet soil, and poor ground balance can make IDs fluctuate. The upgraded detectors reduce some of that uncertainty.

Tip: Don't reject every medium ID. Some gold jewelry can sound like aluminum trash.

Detector control panel

Sensitivity: Higher settings increase detection range but may increase noisy or unstable responses in difficult soil.

Discrimination: Rejects signals below the chosen target ID. It reduces unwanted iron responses but can hide interesting finds. Set it low when searching for historical relics.

Ground balance: Matches the detector to the local soil. Use Auto ground balance when switching locations or changing weather. The ideal value varies by location and conditions.

Ferrous means iron-based; non-ferrous means other conductive metals. Readings are deliberately imperfect for realistic gameplay.

Pinpoint, dig, and recover

When you hear a signal, press P to enter pinpoint mode and narrow down its location. You have 10 seconds of free pinpoint time. Once the timer reaches zero, leaving pinpoint active drains battery at 5% per second before upgrade benefits.

Switching pinpoint off allows the timer to recover gradually; rapidly toggling it will not restore a full allowance.

Stand near the suspected target and press E or Space. After digging, use the handheld pinpointer in the soil recovery dialog and select Recover target.

Upgraded shovels and pinpointers speed up recovery. The battery and pinpoint timer pause while recovery dialogs are open.

Battery management

Standard detecting uses a small amount of power. Pinpointing beyond the free allowance drains it much faster. Watch the battery bar and use pinpoint mode only when needed.

If the battery reaches 0%, you can sell eligible pouch finds and use cash to buy a $10 replacement battery. If you cannot afford it, end the detecting session.

Game Over does not erase your career. Your journal, trophies, upgrades, and recorded discoveries remain saved. Advanced detectors can improve battery efficiency.

Three detecting environments

Residential Yard: beginner targets, modern coins, jewelry and common trash.

Newly Plowed Farm Field: unlock by finding 5 yard coins and recovering 15 yard targets. Look for older coins and farm relics.

Civil War Battlefield: unlock by recovering 20 farm targets and 5 historic farm finds. The setting is fictional and represents authorized private-property detecting.

Expeditions: open the Expeditions menu to see special opportunities. Recover enough targets at a location to unlock its bonus discovery.

Always obtain permission before metal detecting in real life. Never disturb protected historical or archaeological sites.

What to do with discoveries

The Finds Pouch contains recovered items that can be sold for cash. Selling funds equipment and batteries. Every discovery stays recorded in the journal and collection album, even after sale.

In the Treasure Album, mark an unsold find as a trophy to keep it out of pouch sales. Remove the trophy designation later if you want to sell it.

Old coins and jewelry may be appraised for $2. Appraisal reveals an in-game condition or rarity outcome and adjusts the estimated value. These are fictional game valuations, not real-world price quotes.

The Achievements menu tracks collection milestones and lets you claim rewards.

Weather effects

Sunny: normal detection and fast digging. Cloudy: mild soil response. Rainy: stronger range but increased interference and slower digging. Muddy: reduced range, more unstable signals, and slower digging.

Change weather in the detector panel. Recalibrate ground balance after a change for more stable target IDs.

Equipment upgrades

Sell recovered finds to earn cash. Purchase detector upgrades for improved range, target stability, and efficiency; pinpointer upgrades to reduce soil scans; and shovel upgrades for faster digging and experience benefits.

Upgrades cost more at higher levels. Your purchases and collection progress are stored in your local browser save.

There is no required rush to unlock levels. Explore, collect, and upgrade at your own pace.

Save game and backup

The game automatically saves progress in your browser's local storage. Saves are associated with the browser and how the file is opened; they may not follow you to a different browser, device, or file location.

Use Backup / Restore to download a portable JSON save. Keep that file somewhere safe. To move to a new computer or game version, open Backup / Restore and import the JSON.

Import replaces the current save after confirmation. Export your current progress first. The original save key is retained for compatibility with previous versions.

Use New yard / Reset save only if you want to erase progress and begin again.

Common questions

No detector signal? Move slowly, check battery, raise sensitivity, lower discrimination, and try Auto ground balance.

Can't dig? Move closer to the signal and make sure no other dialog is open.

Can't unlock the farm or battlefield? Check the target and coin/relic requirements in the Locations panel.

No sound? Toggle Sound on and interact with the page once; browsers may require a click before audio plays.

Missing progress? Try the same browser and file location used previously. Restore a JSON backup if you have one.

Battery depleted? Sell eligible pouch items and buy a replacement battery, or start a new session.
