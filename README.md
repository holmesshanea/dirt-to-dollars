# 🪙 Dirt to Dollars — Metal Detecting Adventures

**Version 1.0.4** · Free, browser-based metal detecting game

🎮 **[Play Dirt to Dollars](https://holmesshanea.github.io/dirt-to-dollars/)**

Explore three fictional detecting locations, follow your detector's signals, dig for hidden objects, and build a collection of coins, jewelry, and historic relics. Sell finds to upgrade your equipment or keep special discoveries in your trophy collection.

## Play

Open the **[live game](https://holmesshanea.github.io/dirt-to-dollars/)** in a modern desktop or mobile browser. No account, installation, or download is required.

To play offline, download or clone this repository and open `index.html` in your browser.

## Locations

| Location | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| 🏡 Residential Yard | Modern coins, jewelry, aluminum trash, bottle caps, and occasional older finds |
| 🌾 Newly Plowed Farm Field | Farm iron, buckles, buttons, and historic coins |
| 🏕️ Civil War Battlefield | Period relics, buttons, projectiles, buckles, and coins in a fictional authorized detecting setting |

The Farm Field and Battlefield unlock as you meet detecting milestones.

## How to play

1. Move around the map and listen for detector signals.
2. Watch the target ID, tone, signal strength, and estimated depth.
3. Use pinpoint mode to narrow down the target's location.
4. Dig, scan the excavated soil, and recover the item.
5. Sell finds for upgrades, or keep rare items as trophies.

### Controls

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W A S D** / **Arrow keys** | Move the detectorist |
| **P** | Toggle pinpoint mode |
| **E** / **Space** | Dig |
| **H** | Open the Help Center |
| **On-screen buttons** | Move, pinpoint, and dig on touch devices |

## Features

- Top-down exploration with a scrolling, player-following camera
- Three environments with different hidden targets
- Adjustable **sensitivity**, **discrimination**, and **ground balance**
- Detector tones, target ID estimates, depth readings, and soil effects
- Battery management: **10 seconds** of free pinpoint time; extended use drains power
- Interactive digging and handheld pinpointer recovery
- Dynamic weather and soil conditions
- Finds pouch, selling, appraisals, equipment upgrades, and trophy collection
- Finds album, achievements, special expeditions, and career statistics
- Beginner tutorial and searchable in-game Help Center
- Mobile-responsive controls and layout
- Automatic local saves plus JSON backup and restore

## Saving your progress

Progress is saved in your browser's local storage. **Different browsers or devices do not automatically share saves.** Use the in-game **Backup / Restore** feature to export a JSON backup and import it on another device. Clearing browser storage can erase local progress.

## Hosting on GitHub Pages

This is a static HTML game. To publish it using GitHub Pages:

1. Keep `index.html` in the repository root.
2. Open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Choose **Deploy from a branch**, `main`, and `/ (root)`.
4. Save and wait for deployment.

No backend, database, or build process is required.
